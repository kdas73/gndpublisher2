Publish only news with a direct, central connection to Greece.
Reject news where Greece is only mentioned in passing, used for comparison, listed among many countries, or implied only because the source is Greek.
Reject international news unless Greece is explicitly an actor, affected party, host, destination, location, or the article describes concrete consequences for Greece or people/organizations in Greece.
Reject articles whose title and summary do not clearly establish the Greece connection; do not infer missing context.
If the news is international, it is relevant only when Greece is directly involved.
If the article is not clearly about Greece or not directly connected to Greece, mark it as irrelevant.
If the article is about politics (including domestic political process, parties, elections, political disputes, statements of politicians), mark it as irrelevant.
If the article is about death, obituary, passed-away, or similar death-related context, mark it as irrelevant regardless of topic category.
For SPORTS, mark relevant only match results (wins/losses/draws/final score outcomes) and for games with Greek teams and sportsmen. Any other sports content is irrelevant.
